Privacy Policy for KathaPixel Offline PDF Studio

Effective date: 13 November 2026

KathaPixel Offline PDF Studio is an offline Android document utility. Your documents are processed on your device. The developer does not receive them.

Files and processing

You select files using Android's document picker or drop them into the app in split-screen. The app reads those files only for the tool you choose and saves results to a location you select. Some operations make temporary copies in the app's private cache. The app removes these during normal processing; an interrupted operation may leave a temporary copy until the device clears the cache or you clear the app's data. If you select a cloud-backed document provider, that provider may sync files under its own privacy policy.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not send files or personal data to the developer or an app-operated server. It requests no internet permission and contains no advertising or analytics SDK, account system, or in-app purchases. It does not automatically share files with third parties. Opening this policy from the app launches your browser, whose normal request to GitHub Pages is governed by your browser and GitHub's policies.

Security, retention and deletion

Files are handled through Android's document access system and the app's private cache. Android backup of app data is disabled. The app has no account, server-side data, or document library. You control source files and saved results through your file manager or chosen document provider. You can clear the app's cache or data in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to remove any remaining local app data.

Children

The app is a general document utility and is not designed specifically for children.
